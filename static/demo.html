02 / 07
The problem

Data is available. Reliable interpretation is harder.

Environmental trends can be misleading when observations are incomplete, physical units are confused, or uncertainty is ignored.

A graph is a starting point — not a scientific conclusion.

Scientific trust

The Quality Passport questions the result itself.

CoverageWas every requested month available? Which years were excluded?
Method agreementDoes the robust Theil–Sen slope agree with OLS direction?
SensitivityDoes removing one year materially change the estimate?
Evidence limitsAre serial dependence, selection and uncertainty plainly disclosed?

Better Earth decisions begin with better questions.
