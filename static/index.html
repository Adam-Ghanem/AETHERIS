                        THE METHODOLOGY / 003

                        Science before spectacle.

                        Every AETHERIS finding is linked to its data and its assumptions.

                        01 / DATA

                        NASA POWER meteorological reanalysis

                        We query historical monthly NASA POWER data at selected coordinates. Air temperature (T2M) and corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR) are gridded, model-derived environmental products, not local station observations or high-resolution maps.

                        NASA POWER documentation
                        02 / AGGREGATION

                        From monthly values to annual signals

                        Temperature: calendar-day-weighted mean over 12 complete months. Precipitation: sum of monthly mm/day rates multiplied by the days of each month, giving an estimated annual total in mm. Incomplete years are excluded and reported.

                        03 / INFERENCE

                        Regression, uncertainty and robustness

                        Ordinary least squares estimates change per decade. Heteroskedasticity- and autocorrelation-consistent (Newey–West/HAC) standard errors support an exploratory two-sided significance assessment. A Theil–Sen robust slope offers a second perspective.

                        Confidence intervals and p-values depend on method assumptions and temporal coverage. A small p-value is not proof that a specific driver caused the change.

                        04 / MULTI-VARIABLE

                        Coupled Earth-system signals

                        Earth Systems Lab aligns the same complete calendar years of NASA POWER T2M and PRECTOTCORR. Two-source anomalies and correlations are descriptive. Annual co-occurrence is not proof of drought, climate attribution, or a causal relationship.

                        05 / BREAKPOINTS

                        Exploratory changes in trend rate

                        Change-point Lab fits a continuous hinge regression at observed candidate years, compares it against one linear slope by BIC, and reports leave-one-year-out stability. Searching the same series for the best split makes ordinary post-selection significance tests invalid; no formal breakpoint significance is claimed.

                        06 / TIME MACHINE

                        Complete-year anomalies at nine points

                        Each point is a discrete NASA POWER reanalysis sample. A year is displayed only when all 12 monthly observations exist. The reference window is the first ten calendar years in the request; its mean is computed separately for each point with at least eight complete reference years. The timeline is descriptive and is not a continuous spatial raster.

                        07 / REPRODUCIBILITY

                        Inspect and export the evidence

                        The evidence ledger records source URL, location, variable, range, aggregation, calculation method and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the observations. Export complete investigation JSON or annual CSV to replicate calculations.
